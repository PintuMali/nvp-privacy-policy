NVP Accounting – Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 2025  |  App: NVP Accounting  |  Bundle ID: com.nvp.accounting

Summary

NVP Accounting does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All data you enter stays on your device.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No account registration or sign-in
  • No names, email addresses, or contact information
  • No financial figures or business data leave your device
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or telemetry
  • No advertising identifiers or tracking
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data

Local Storage Only

All accounting records, databases, and exported files (PDF/CSV) are stored exclusively in your device's local app sandbox (Documents/). Nothing is uploaded to any server.

File Access

The app reads and writes files only within its own sandboxed Documents/ directory. It can also import a database file you explicitly select via the iOS Files picker — this file is copied locally and the original is not modified.

System APIs Used (Required Reasons)

The app accesses the following iOS system APIs solely for core functionality:

  • File timestamps — to display file modification dates (reasons C617.1, 3B52.1)
  • Disk space — to check available storage before writing exports (reason E174.1)
  • UserDefaults — to store app preferences locally on-device (reason CA92.1)

These are declared in the app's PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy manifest.

Encryption

The app does not implement any custom encryption. The app password is a local access control mechanism only. ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption is set to false.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If the privacy practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date.
